Two Heavens will lead you from the river shore to the blade of a katana.

You will master the art of swordsmithing in the traditional Japanese way.

You will learn the way of the warrior: to wield the katana and wakizashi as one, in the Niten Ichi Ryu, the technique of Two Heavens.

<h1 align="center">How to Make A Sword</h1>

Mine Satetsu from sand next to any body of water.

Combine Sugar Cane, Charcoal and Clay in a crafting table to get Tatara Clay.

Combine four Tatara Clay in a crafting table to make a Tatara Clay Block.

Combine eight Tatara Clay Blocks in a Furnace pattern in a crafting table to make an Unfired Tatara Furnace.

Combine Wood Planks of any type, one Iron Ingot and two Leather in a crafting table to make a Bellows.

Combine six Cobblestone and one Stick in a crafting table to make a Hammer.

Place the Unfired Tatara Furnace on ground.

Right-click on the Unfired Tatara Furnace with eight Charcoal to fill it layer by layer until full.

Right-click on the Unfired Tatara Furnace with Flint and Steel to light the Charcoal inside it.

Wait one minute until the Unfired Tatara Furnace becomes brown and converts into a Fired Tatara Furnace.

Right-click on the fired Tatara Furnace with four Charcoal to fill it layer by layer until half full. 

Then Right-click on the half full Fired Tatara Furnace with four Satetsu to fill it layer by layer until full.

Wait thirty seconds for the Satetsu to become hot but not hot enough to smelt. Indicated by the Satetsu not smoking anymore.

Then stoke the Satetsu with bellows every five seconds for thirty seconds until it is hot enough to smelt.

The Satetsu will make a whooshing sound once it has finished smelting and successfully turned into Kera.

Right-click four times with a hammer on the Fired Tatara Furnace with Kera in it to break Furnace.

It will leave behind the Molten Kera.

Wait until the Molten Kera cools and turns into Kera, then mine it with a stone pickaxe.

Reheat the cool Kera in a Furnace for ten seconds to turn it into Hot Kera.

Combine three Iron Ingots in a diagonal line in a crafting table to make Tongs.

Combine seven Iron Ingots in an anvil shape in a crafting table to make a Smithing Anvil.

Place Smithing Anvil and Right-click on it with the Hot Kera to place the Hot Kera on the anvil.

At any point before the Hot Kera becomes a blade, Right-click on it with an empty hand to return the current item to your inventory, and Right-click on the anvil with that item again to put it back and resume forging.

Right-click two times with a Hammer on the Hot Kera on the Smithing Anvil to shrink the Hot Kera.

Hammer the shrunk Hot Kera a third time to flatten it into a Tamahagane Ingot.

Hammer the Tamahagane Ingot a fourth time to flatten it further into a Flat Tamahagane Ingot.

Hammer the Flat Tamahagane Ingot a fifth time to flatten it further into a Hot Wakizashi Blade.

Hammer the Hot Wakizashi Blade a sixth time to flatten it further into a Hot Katana Blade.

Equip Tongs in offhand and Right-click on anvil with empty mainhand to equip Hot Wakizashi Blade or Hot Katana Blade in mainhand. 

If you stop holding the Tongs, the hot blade will drop and burn you and keep on doing it until you re-equip the Tongs.

With Tongs in offhand and hot blade in mainhand, Right-click on water to quench hot blade and turn it into a Wakizashi or Katana Blade.

Combine four Iron Ingots in a crafting table to make a Tsuba.

Combine three Sticks and six Leather in a crafting table to make a Katana Tsuka.

Combine two Sticks and four Leather in a crafting table to make a Wakizashi Tsuka.

Combine Tsuba, Katana or Wakizashi Tsuka and Katana or Wakizashi Blade in a crafting table to make a Katana or Wakizashi.
