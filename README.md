Two Heavens will lead you from the river shore to the blade of a katana.

You will master the art of swordsmithing in the traditional Japanese way.

You will learn the way of the warrior: to wield two swords in the Niten Ichi Ryu, the technique of Two Heavens.

<h1 align="center">How to Make A Sword</h1>

Mine Satetsu from sand next to any body of water.

Gather Sugar Cane, Charcoal and Clay.

Combine them in a crafting table to get Tatara Clay.

Combine four Tatara Clay in a crafting table to make a Tatara Clay Block.

Combine eight Tatara Clay Blocks in a Furnace pattern in a crafting table to make an Unfired Tatara Furnace.

Gather six Wood Planks of any type, one Iron Ingot and three Leather.

Combine them in a crafting table to make a Bellows.

Gather six Cobblestone and one Stick.

Combine them in a Furnace to make a Hammer.

Place the Unfired Tatara Furnace on ground.

Click on the Unfired Tatara Furnace with eight Charcoal to fill it layer by layer until full.

Click on the Unfired Tatara Furnace with Flint and Steel to light the Charcoal inside it.

Wait one minute until the Unfired Tatara Furnace becomes brown and converts into a Fired Tatara Furnace.

Wait 30 seconds for the Charcoal to become hot but not hot enough to smelt. Indicated by fire not smoking anymore.

Then stoke the Charcoal with bellows every five seconds for one minute until it is hot enough to smelt.
